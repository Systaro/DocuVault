### Word documents open in the browser

A .docx file in a space now opens as pages you can read, instead of a card
that asks you to download it. Headings, tables, lists, images, headers and
footers show the way Word lays them out, and meeting transcripts exported
from Teams read at their normal size.

Files DocuVault cannot read still offer the download.
