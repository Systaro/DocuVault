The fullscreen preview now has a close button in the top right corner that
stays on screen, and Escape leaves the preview as well. Until now the only way
back was an arrow in a top bar that hides itself and only slides in when the
cursor touches the very top edge, so many people found no way out at all. The
close button replaces that arrow.

Leaving takes you to what you were looking at: the document in the editor, or
the space overview for a folder. If you switched to another file inside the
preview, you land on that one. Escape first closes whatever is open on top,
such as an enlarged image or diagram, a comment or a dialog, and the next press
leaves the preview.
