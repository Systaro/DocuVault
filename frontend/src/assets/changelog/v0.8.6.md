Comments written in Word now show when you open a .docx file. The words a
comment refers to are highlighted, and clicking them opens the comment next to
the text with its author, date and replies. Comments resolved in Word are
highlighted in green. The arrows on a comment, or the counter at the bottom of
the page, take you from one comment to the next.

The comments come from the file itself, so they are read-only here: to reply
or resolve one, open the file in Word. Everyone who can open the file sees its
comments, the same as in Word.
