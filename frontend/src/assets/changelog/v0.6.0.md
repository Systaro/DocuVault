Comments now start where the conversation does: in the text itself.

### Say it about the exact words

Select any passage in a document and right-click it. *Comment on selection*
attaches your remark to those words — not to a spot on the page — so it keeps
pointing at the right sentence after the document is rewritten around it. The
passage stays marked in the document, and clicking the mark opens the thread.

![Selecting a passage and commenting on it from the right-click menu](demo:comment-on-selection)

The same menu works on a shared link when the link allows commenting, so a
reviewer who does not have an account can still point at a sentence and say
what they mean. A view-only link says so instead of offering an action that
would fail.

### Pages that keep working while you edit them

An HTML page built around a tab row used to go dead the moment you opened it
for editing: its scripts were off, so the tabs did nothing and the content
behind them was out of reach. A page can now mark those parts with
`data-dv-interactive`, and they stay live while the prose around them is
edited — and they are saved exactly as they are in the file, whatever their
scripts did on screen.

![A tab row staying live while the page around it is edited](assets/changelog/v0.6.0/interactive-regions.png)

### Editing a long page with AI no longer times out

Asking the AI to fix something in a large page used to hang for a minute and
then fail. It now sends back the specific edits it wants to make instead of
rewriting the whole file, which is both far quicker and far less likely to
reformat parts you never mentioned. Instructions that genuinely rewrite a whole
document still do, and no longer run out of time.

![The AI returning two targeted edits instead of the whole file](assets/changelog/v0.6.0/ai-edit-patch.png)

### Also

- Shared HTML pages load the stylesheets, scripts and images they reference
  from outside the shared folder, so a design shared on its own is no longer
  unstyled.
- HTML files, PDFs and images show who created them and who last changed them,
  and an HTML file has the same version history and time capsule a document has.
- The right-click menu no longer fails to open on Linux.
- Administrators can put someone into their teams while inviting them, so
  access is ready before the person signs up.
