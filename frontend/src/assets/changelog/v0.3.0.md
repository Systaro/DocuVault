Space access can finally be granted per team instead of per person, and
git-backed spaces now autosave while you type.

### Teams

Users inherit every space grant their teams hold — across search, inbox,
notifications, digest emails and the MCP server. Manage them under
*Admin → Teams*.

![Teams](assets/changelog/v0.3.0/teams.png)

### On-this-page outline

Long documents get a navigation rail built from their H1/H2 headings —
click to jump to a section, and the rail highlights where you are while
you scroll.

![On-this-page outline](assets/changelog/v0.3.0/outline.png)

### Creator & last editor

The document topbar now shows who created the document and who last
edited it, each with date and time, plus a quick link into the version
history.

![Creator and last editor in the topbar](assets/changelog/v0.3.0/topbar.png)

### Editing

- Autosave in git-backed spaces: edits persist continuously while typing;
  Save/Done makes the commit
- Highlight colors in the editor that survive the markdown round-trip

![Highlight colors](assets/changelog/v0.3.0/highlights.png)

### Fixes & polish

- This *"What's new"* dialog — release notes shown once after login
- Delete/move dialogs got proper buttons; delete shows a busy spinner and
  closes on failure instead of hanging
- Ghost entries are gone: documents whose file left the working tree are
  pruned automatically
